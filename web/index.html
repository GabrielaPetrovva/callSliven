How it works

Four steps to a better street

You don't need to know which institution is responsible. We take it from there.

  1. 1

    Register

    Create a profile and confirm your email.

  2. 2

    Submit a report

    Describe the problem, mark it on the map and add photos.

  3. 3

    We forward it

    An administrator reviews the report and sends it to the responsible institution.

  4. 4

    Track the status

    Every change is public until the problem is resolved.

Why report

One report makes a difference

It goes to the right people, and everyone can see what happens next.

  • Reaches the right institution

    The Municipality, ViK, EVN, RIOSV and others. You don't need to know who is responsible.

  • Transparent for everyone

    Reports and their status are public.

  • Your personal data is protected

    Your name, email, phone and photos are not published, and the location is shown only approximately.

  • No paperwork or queues

    Report from your phone, wherever you see the problem, at any time.
